More buyers now ask an AI assistant "what's the best tool for…" before they ever visit a website. If the answer names your competitors and not you, you've lost that buyer before you knew they existed. Riposte checks this for you in two steps.

## Step 1: Prompt Studio writes the questions buyers ask

You give Prompt Studio your keywords and, if you like, the niches you want to win. It writes about 60 buying questions, grouped into two kinds of topics:

- **Shield topics:** broad category questions, like "best competitive intelligence tool".
- **Spear topics:** niches you want to win, each a product plus a situation, like "competitor alerts for content teams".

Every prompt asks for a recommendation, never names a brand, and covers a different angle: who is asking, for what, and with which limits. Riposte checks each prompt against these rules and removes any that break them. You can export them as a CSV for Profound.

![Prompt Studio](/blog/screens/prompts.jpg "Prompt Studio: buying questions grouped into Shield and Spear topics")


## Step 2: AI visibility asks them and records the answers

Pick the prompts that matter most and click **Ask**. Riposte asks each one in Gemini, the way a buyer would, and records:

- **Which products the answer names**, and in what order.
- **Which sites it relied on**, when web search is on.
- **The searches the AI ran** to answer.

You see your share of answers next to each competitor's, and the most-cited sites. Those are the places to get listed on. Prompts are asked again every week, so you can watch the trend.

![AI visibility results](/blog/screens/visibility.jpg "Share of AI answers per product, and the sites the answers relied on")


## Step 3: close the gap

The most useful view is **Competitors named, not you**. For each gap, **Why are they winning?** shows what those competitors changed in the last 30 days, such as a rewritten comparison page or new answer pages, and the sites the answer leaned on. One click writes a content brief to win that answer.
