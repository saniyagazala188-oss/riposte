## What's in a signal

Each signal has three parts:

- **What changed**: the facts, from the competitor's own page
- **Why it matters to you**: judged against [your product](/docs/your-product)
- **What to do**: the next step

It also shows the **impact** (high, medium or low) and the **type**: pricing, product, content, positioning or news.

## The feed views

- **To review**: new signals you haven't handled yet
- **All**: everything except noise
- **Noise**: small changes Riposte filtered out

## Handle a signal

- **Mark reviewed** when you've seen it.
- **Dismiss** when it doesn't matter.
- **Build action kit** to turn it into a response with owners and drafts.
