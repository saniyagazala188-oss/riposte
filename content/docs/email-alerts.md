## Turn on email alerts

1. Open **Alerts & account**.
2. Tick **Email me high-impact changes**.
3. Tick **Weekly digest every Monday**.
4. Click **Save**.

## Test it

Under **Try it**, click **Send a test email**, or **Send this week's digest now** to see a full summary.

:::note Not arriving?
Check your spam folder, and add Riposte to your contacts so future alerts land in your inbox.
:::
