## Sign in

Use **Continue with Google**, or enter your email for a login link. To log in with a password instead, open **Alerts & account** and set one.

## Beta limits

![Beta limits: 5 competitors, 10 prompts, 10 files](/docs/account/limits.jpg)

## Start fresh

In **Alerts & account**, **Clear all history** deletes every signal, action item, connected move and trend. Competitors and their pages stay, and tracking carries on from today.

## Give feedback

Open **Give feedback** in the app. It takes two minutes, and every answer is read.

## Your data

You can delete files, competitors and history yourself. To delete your whole account, see the [privacy policy](/privacy).
