## Write a page

1. Open **Comparisons**.
2. Pick a competitor from the list.
3. Click **Write the page**.

Each page has a criteria table with pricing first, who should choose which, a verdict and FAQs.

![Pick a competitor and write the page](/docs/compare/write.jpg)

## Keep it true

Each page in the list shows **up to date**, **out of date** or **not written**. When a page is out of date, open it to see what changed, then click **Update the page**. The rows that changed are highlighted.

![An out-of-date page with the update button](/docs/compare/update.jpg)

## Put it on your site

Click **Copy as Markdown** and paste it into your website or CMS. Fill in any **[placeholders]** first.
