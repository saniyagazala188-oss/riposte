## Write prompts

1. Open **Prompt Studio**. Fill in [your product](/docs/your-product) first if you haven't.
2. Under **Keywords you want to win**, add at least two keywords, one per line.
3. Optional: add **Areas to win**, niches where you want AI to recommend you.
4. Click **Write prompts**.

![Keywords turned into buyer prompts](/docs/prompts/write.jpg)

## Track the ones that matter

Prompts are grouped into **Shield** topics (broad category questions) and **Spear** topics (niches you want to win). Click **Track** on up to 10. Tracked prompts are asked in [AI visibility](/docs/ai-visibility).

![Click Track on the prompts that matter](/docs/prompts/track.jpg)

## Export

Click **Download CSV (Profound-ready)** to use the prompts in Profound or a spreadsheet.
