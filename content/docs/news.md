Big competitor moments often happen outside their website: a funding round, an analyst award, an outage. Riposte catches those too.

## How it works

Every morning, Riposte searches the news for each competitor by name. Stories are grouped, explained for your product, and sorted by type.

![News sorted into launches, partnerships, awards, funding, leadership and trouble](/docs/news/tabs.jpg)

## Find a story

1. Open **News & more**.
2. Pick a type, such as **Awards & recognition** or **Funding**.
3. Pick a competitor to see only their stories.

:::tip Awards are sales moments
When a competitor wins an award, buyers will ask about it. The signal suggests what sales can say back.
:::
