## Before you start

Track a few prompts in [Prompt Studio](/docs/prompt-studio) first. AI visibility asks those prompts.

## Read the results

Open **AI visibility**. You'll see:

- **Share of AI answers**: how many answers name each product, you included
- **Sites AI relies on**: the sources behind the answers
- **Each prompt's answer**: which products it named and in what order

![Share of AI answers and the sites AI relies on](/docs/visibility/share.jpg)

## Close a gap

When competitors are named and you aren't:

1. Click **Why are they winning?** to see the reasons from that answer.
2. Click **Write a content brief to win this answer** to get a brief.

![A gap with the reasons and a brief to win it](/docs/visibility/gap.jpg)

Click **Run again now** any time to ask all tracked prompts again.
