## Read the overview

Open **Content intel**. You'll see:

- **At a glance**: how often each competitor publishes and what kinds of pages
- **Trending across competitors**: topics two or more of them are writing about
- **Recently published**: their newest posts
- **Content briefs**: briefs you've created

![Publishing pace and trends across competitors](/docs/content/overview.jpg)

## Turn a trend into a brief

1. Find a trend under **Trending across competitors**. No trends yet? Click **Find trends**.
2. Click **Write a content brief**.

The brief gives you a target keyword, the angle to take, a quick answer, an outline and FAQs, ready to hand to a writer.

![A content brief with keyword, angle and outline](/docs/content/brief.jpg)

:::tip Briefs from AI answers too
In [AI visibility](/docs/ai-visibility), you can write a brief to win an AI answer where competitors are named and you aren't.
:::
