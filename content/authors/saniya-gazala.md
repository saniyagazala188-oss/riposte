## About me

I'm Saniya. I've spent six years moving between building things and explaining them. I started out writing code, then fell into content and SEO, and spent my last role as the product marketing manager for KaneAI at TestMu AI (LambdaTest).

Riposte came straight out of that job. Every Monday I spent two to three hours checking what competitors had changed, by hand, in spreadsheets. Even when I found something important, nothing told me what to do about it. So I built the tool I wanted: one that checks every morning, explains what matters for my product and drafts the response.

## My learning journey

- **2020:** finished my B.Tech in Computer Science and started as a web development and WordPress intern.
- **2020 to 2023:** worked as a software engineer, then as a web solutions manager at a four-person startup, writing specs, testing features and writing the help docs.
- **2023 to 2026:** moved into technical content writing at TestMu AI and learned SEO by doing it, then grew into the product marketing manager for KaneAI: comparison pages, sales enablement, launches and AI search.
- **2026:** started building marketing tools myself. I finished Profound's Marketing Engineering course, built and launched Riposte, and I'm now learning performance marketing and AEO/GEO.
